# Config-E — Naturalistic-Frame Clearing: Findings

*Draft findings section for the Nucleation Pilot write-up. Benign open-weight lane; frozen detector `nucleation-detector-1.1.0` (SHA-256 `6094de97…a2934`), byte-identical across all runs. Pre-registered in PREREGISTRATION_AMENDMENT v0.17–v0.19 (commit-before-run). Full numbers: `CONFIG_E_naturalistic_frame_RESULT.md`; provenance: DESIGN_LOG C15–C19.*

## Summary

Config-E asks whether the frozen decision-aligned "clearing" axis — validated on the owned toy (held-out AUC 0.807) and shown to transfer, source-decoupled, to six open-weight families on a *designed* retraction premise (Stage-3a, C13–C14) — still reads when the manipulation is a **naturalistic conversational frame the model infers from the user's own words**, in-context, with no fine-tuning. The seed is a benign weekend-planning opener ("I always get into trouble when I go out") that a range of deployed assistants spontaneously inflate into an alcohol-risk frame that then steers later, topically-unrelated turns. Config-E takes that black-box behavior white-box: does a benign seeded frame leave a residual-stream trace that (a) is readable, (b) survives an explicit correction, and (c) is causally source-decoupled from the seed turn — across independently-built models the detector was never fit to?

The headline: **the representational signal is robust and generalizes; its causal wiring is predominantly — but not universally — source-decoupled.**

## Method (as run)

The read is the unmodified Stage-3a machinery: `detector_frozen.directional_residue` / `paired_effect_size` / `paired_minimal_test`, at layer-fraction 0.6, n = 24 minimal pairs per family. The design is a **paired minimal pair on the seed**: `seed_i` and `noseed_i` are byte-identical conversations except the trouble clause (or, in the covert variant, a hidden note), so `delta_i = seed_i − noseed_i` cancels topic, structure, and the presence of the correction turn — the group-level confound that defeated the first (v2) read (`base_auc ≈ 0.90` with no seed). Three paired quantities per variant: `trace_live` (does the seed leave a trace, no correction), `trace_cleared` (does it survive the correction — re-attachment), and a same-condition null control (no-seed vs shuffled no-seed, required non-significant). A behavioral frame-shift gate (G1) guards against circularity and is scoped to the overt variant only.

The causal leg (H-E2) went through three pre-registered estimator revisions, each committed before its confirmatory run, with the detector never changing: v3.1 fixed a backwards null control; v3.2 replaced a saturated binary "survives" flag with a continuous effect-size ratio `ρ = d_masked / d_cleared` on a surgical seed-clause mask; v3.3 replaced a brittle efficacy-ratio guard with a **projection-variance-stability guard** (score only if masking does not inflate the paired-projection spread past 2.5×) and a **content-matched decoy** (the non-source control is an equal-length contentful clause selected to match the seed clause's masking efficacy, so "does masking a different, equally-strong clause also collapse the signal?" is a fair question). Under v3.3, a variant is **source-decoupled** when the clearing signal survives cutting the read's path to the seed clause *and* to the matched decoy (both ρ ≥ 0.5, both variance-stable); **source-coupled** when masking the seed clause specifically collapses it while the matched decoy does not; **undetermined** otherwise.

Four variants were run across all families: V0 (overt self-disclosed frame), V1 (covert plant — the prompt-injection threat model, seed moved out of the user's mouth), V3 (standardized neutral cue), V6 (steer-flip — separates a nucleated frame from the model's safety prior). Six families: Qwen2.5-1.5B, Phi-3.5-mini, SmolLM2-1.7B, Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen2.5-7B, OLMo-2-1124-7B (the two 7B models in 4-bit, consistent with Stage-3 practice).

## Result 1 — the representational core transfers completely (24/24 cells)

In every family and every variant, the seed leaves a readable paired trace (`readable_live` true throughout), the same-condition null control is dead (0/24 wins, p = 1.0), and the trace **persists through the correction** (`persists` true throughout). A benign seeded frame therefore leaves a confound-controlled, readable residual-stream signature that survives an explicit user walk-back, in all six open-weight families — generalizing the Stage-3a clearing read from a designed premise to a naturalistic in-context frame with no fine-tuning. This is the robust "still-in-there" result and it is unconditional across the tested substrate.

Whether the correction *intensifies* the trace (re-attachment, `clearing_index ≤ 0`) or *partly clears* it is model- and variant-dependent and does not track model size: Llama-3.2-3B re-attaches on all four variants, while SmolLM2-1.7B and both 7B models (Qwen-7B, OLMo-7B) partly clear on the overt/neutral variants while retaining a large, significant residual. Persistence is the universal claim; the sign of the correction's effect is a per-model property.

## Result 2 — source-decoupling is predominant but not universal (H-E2)

Under the v3.3 causal leg, the clearing residue is **source-decoupled in 19 of 24 family×variant cells**, with 4 coupled and 1 undetermined. At the family level, five of six models are predominantly source-decoupled — SmolLM2, Llama, and Qwen-7B are decoupled on all four variants; Qwen-1.5B and Phi are majority-decoupled. This matches the direction of the Stage-3a causal-ablation result (6/6 decoupled on the designed premise) and meets the registered ≥4/6-family threshold. The honest qualifier is that decoupling is *predominant, not universal*.

**OLMo-2-7B is a genuine outlier.** It source-*couples* on two of four variants — V1 (covert plant, ρ_src 0.48) and V6 (flip, ρ_src 0.47) — both variance-stable and content-matched-decoy-clean (V6's decoy ρ is 4.79: masking an equally-strong different clause *raises* the effect while masking the seed clause halves it, a textbook seed-specific dependence). OLMo is the one family whose causal character is an even split rather than predominantly decoupled, and it is what corrects an earlier within-run impression that the flip variant decoupled everywhere: V6 is source-decoupled in 5 of 6 families, not all six.

## Result 3 — the coupling is structured, not noise

The four coupled cells are not randomly scattered. They cluster by variant in an interpretable way: **the overt variant (V0) never couples** (source-decoupled in all five families where it scores; undetermined only in Qwen-1.5B, whose overt seed mask inflates projection variance past the stability guard), whereas **the covert plant (V1) is the most coupling-prone**, coupling in two of six families (Phi, OLMo). V3 and V6 each couple once. Coupling is also unstable across scale within an architecture: Qwen-1.5B couples on the neutral cue (V3) while Qwen-7B, the same family scaled up, decouples on it. The reading this supports — offered as a hypothesis for a dedicated follow-up, not a closed finding — is that an *overtly self-disclosed* frame's clearing residue reliably rides downstream positions (decoupled from its origin), while a *covertly planted* frame is the most likely to remain tied to its own tokens in some models.

## Result 4 — the frozen axis is not a deployable behavioral monitor here (H-E3)

The still-steering monitor test (does the per-turn axis projection predict the behavioral outcome the surface wording has not yet revealed) is a clean null: axis-monitor accuracy sat at chance/floor against a caution-word lexicon baseline in every variant tested (recorded at v3.1). The frozen clearing axis reads an internal state; it does not, on this naturalistic frame and with this lexicon interface, function as a live behavioral predictor that beats a trivial word-count. This is reported as a negative result, independent of the representational and causal findings.

## Relation to published white-box deception detection

The closest published instrument to the frozen detector is Anthropic's sleeper-agent probe: a **linear read of residual-stream activations** that flags when a deceptively-aligned model will defect, reaching >99% AUROC from tiny contrast pairs. That work's stated caveat is the motivation for Config-E — the authors note the linear salience of defection may be an artifact of their *artificially-created* sleeper agents, and whether *naturally* deceptive models carry the same linearly-readable signal is left as an open question. Config-E speaks directly to that gap: it takes a same-family linear read to a **naturalistically inferred, in-context frame** (not a trained-in trigger) and shows the trace is readable, persistent, and predominantly source-decoupled across **six independently-built open-weight families the detector was never fit to**. This is evidence on the generalization axis the probe literature flags as unresolved — with the honest boundary that our manipulation is a benign premise-clearing analogue, not a trained-in deceptive objective (see Scope below).

## Scope and limitations

This is the benign open-weight lane throughout: the manipulation is premise-clearing and frame-inference, the safe analogue of refusal-neutralization; the safety-target version (real refusal-breach residue) requires controlled elicitation on owned models in a private venue and is out of scope here by design. The result rests on n = 24 pairs per family at a single layer and a single read. The behavioral scorers behind G1 and the monitor are lexicon-based, which is the weak link in the interface and the natural place for a rubric-judge upgrade. H-E2 is predominant, not universal — OLMo demonstrates that a whole family can source-couple half its variants — so the transfer claim is stated as *predominant source-decoupling with model- and variant-specific coupling*, and the coupling structure (overt-never, covert-most, scale-unstable) is a hypothesis the data raise rather than one it settles.

## One-line statement

A benign, naturalistically-inferred conversational frame leaves a readable, confound-controlled residual-stream trace that persists through an explicit correction in all six open-weight families tested; that trace is predominantly (5/6 families, 19/24 cells) source-decoupled from the seed turn — the same direction as the designed-premise result — with coupling confined to covert plants and one 7B family, and with the frozen axis not functioning as a deployable behavioral monitor on this frame.
